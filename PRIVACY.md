# Privacy

Thread Status runs entirely on your own computer.

- **What it reads:** the Claude desktop app's session files on your computer, to get each thread's title and timing.
- **What it keeps:** one note per thread in the plugin's own store on your computer, holding the time and the closing words of that thread's last answer, and only when those words ask you something. Notes older than 3 days are removed.
- **What it sends:** nothing. It makes no network requests and has no server. No one, including the author, receives any of your data.
- **How to remove it:** uninstall the plugin. Its store is removed with your plugin data.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Tereseus/thread-status/issues
